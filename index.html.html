Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 25, 2026

Summary: Board Game Timer does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. The app works entirely offline on your device.

1. Introduction

Board Game Timer ("we", "our", or "us") is a board game timer application developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. By using Rummikub Timer, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Rummikub Timer does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (beyond what advertising SDKs may access – see Section 4)
  • Game history or usage logs sent to external servers
  • Microphone, camera, or storage data

All game data (player names, timer settings) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Local Data Storage

The app may save your last-used player names and settings locally on your device using Android's standard storage (SharedPreferences / Room database). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rummikub Timer displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain non-personal device information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID)
  • Device type and OS version
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)
  • General location (country/region, not precise GPS)

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy. You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Children's Privacy

Rummikub Timer is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed for general audiences (families and adults) playing board games.

6. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party SDK:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements (AdMob Privacy Policy)

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

7. Data Security

Since Rummikub Timer does not transmit user data to any server, there is no risk of a server-side data breach. Local device data is protected by Android's built-in security mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
